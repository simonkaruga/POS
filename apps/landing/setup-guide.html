Setup Guide

From account creation to your first KRA-compliant sale — follow these 8 steps to get Faida POS running for your business.

~20 minutes to complete
No technical knowledge required
Updated March 2026

1
Create Your Account
Sign up and register your business — takes 2 minutes

Visit /setup and complete the business registration form. You'll need:

  • Business name — as registered with the Registrar of Companies
  • Your email address — this becomes your Owner login
  • Phone number — for account verification and M-Pesa notifications
  • Password — minimum 8 characters

After registration, you'll be logged in automatically as the Owner — the highest-privilege role. Only the Owner can access Settings, add users, and configure integrations.

💡

Tip: You get a 30-day free trial with full access to all features on any plan. No credit card is needed to start — you can explore everything before committing.

2
Configure Business Details
Settings → General — your business profile and tax settings

Go to Settings → General and fill in your business information:

  • Business Name & Address — appears on every receipt
  • Store Name & Phone — shown on receipts and the customer pole display
  • Tax Type: choose VAT (16%) if VAT-registered, or TOT (3%) for turnover tax. This controls how tax is calculated and displayed on receipts.
  • POS Mode: RETAIL for standard shops, RESTAURANT for hospitality (tables, KOT), PHARMACY for prescription tracking
  • Receipt Header & Footer — optional custom messages printed on every receipt
  • Service Charge: enable for restaurants (default 10%)

Click Save Settings. Changes apply immediately to all new receipts.

⚠️

Important: Your tax type (VAT vs TOT) affects eTIMS submissions. Set this correctly before processing your first sale. To change it later you'll need to contact KRA support to update your eTIMS profile.

3
Configure KRA eTIMS
Settings → KRA eTIMS — mandatory for VAT-registered businesses

Faida submits every invoice to KRA automatically. To enable this, you need credentials from the KRA iTax portal:

  1. Log in to itax.kra.go.ke
  2. Navigate to eTIMS → Device Management → Register Device
  3. Register a new Virtual Sales Control Unit (VSCU)
  4. Note your BHF ID (Branch ID), TIN (your KRA PIN), and CMC Key

Then in Faida, go to Settings → KRA eTIMS and enter:

  • BHF ID — your Branch ID from KRA (e.g. 000 for head office)
  • TIN — your KRA PIN (e.g. P051234567M)
  • CMC Key — the device communication key from KRA

Click Save & Test Connection. Faida will verify the credentials with KRA. Once confirmed, every checkout will auto-submit to eTIMS.

✅

What happens after each sale: Faida submits the invoice in the background. The receipt prints with a KRA control number. If the internet is down, the invoice is queued and submitted automatically when connectivity is restored — the receipt shows a "PENDING KRA SUBMISSION" watermark until then.

4
Configure M-Pesa
Settings → M-Pesa — Safaricom Daraja API credentials

To accept M-Pesa payments (STK Push and QR), you need a Safaricom Daraja API account:

  1. Register at developer.safaricom.co.ke
  2. Create an app and go live (requires Safaricom business verification)
  3. Obtain your Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, Business Shortcode, and Passkey

In Faida go to Settings → M-Pesa and enter these credentials. Your M-Pesa keys are encrypted with AES-256-CBC before storage — they are never accessible in plaintext after saving.

Don't have Daraja yet? You can still use Faida — M-Pesa is optional. Cash, bank transfer, Paystack card, and gift cards work without Daraja credentials. You can add M-Pesa later once your Daraja account is approved.

5
Add Your Inventory
Inventory → Items — manual entry or bulk CSV import

Add your products and services. There are two ways:

Option A — Manual Entry

Click Add Item and fill in:

  • Item Name & Category
  • Retail Price (KES) — always enter inclusive of tax
  • Cost Price — for profit margin calculation
  • Barcode — scan with a USB scanner or camera, or type manually
  • Track Stock: enable to deduct quantity on each sale
  • Initial Stock Quantity — if tracking stock

Option B — CSV Import (recommended for 10+ items)

Click Import CSV. Download the template, fill it with your items, and upload. The importer handles hundreds of items in seconds.

Required columns: name, category, retailPriceCents, costPriceCents. Optional: barcode, sku, trackStock, initialStock.

💡

Prices in cents: The CSV uses cents (integer) to avoid decimal rounding. KES 150.00 = 15000. KES 299.50 = 29950.

6
Add Staff Users
Settings → Users — create logins for your team

Faida has 4 built-in roles with different permission levels:

  • Owner — full access: settings, users, reports, all data
  • Manager — POS, inventory, reports, HR; cannot change business settings
  • Cashier — POS only; logs in with a 4-digit PIN for fast checkout
  • Waiter — hospitality mode only; manages tables and orders

To add a user: go to Settings → Users → Add User. For cashiers, set a 4-digit PIN — they use PIN login at the POS instead of email/password for speed.

🔐

Security tip: Never share the Owner account with cashiers. Create individual accounts for each staff member — this enables accurate shift reports, commission tracking, and a clean audit trail per employee.

For multi-store businesses, use the Store Assignment modal to restrict managers and cashiers to their specific branch.

7
Start Selling
POS — the main sales screen

Click POS in the left navigation. The POS screen has three zones: item grid (left), cart (middle), payment (right).

Adding Items to Cart

  • Click any item in the grid to add 1 unit
  • Barcode scanner: scan the product barcode — auto-adds to cart
  • Camera scan: click the camera icon to use your device camera as a scanner
  • Quick quantity: type 3×500 in the search bar to add 3 items priced at KES 5.00

Keyboard Shortcuts

KeyAction
F1Open payment modal (checkout)
F2Focus search / barcode field
F3Add customer to sale
F4Apply discount to cart
F5Hold / park current sale
F8Void / clear cart

Processing Payment

Press F1 or click Charge KES X. Choose payment method:

  • Cash — enter amount received, system calculates change
  • M-Pesa STK Push — enter customer phone, they receive a push on their phone
  • M-Pesa QR — customer scans the on-screen QR code
  • Card (Paystack) — customer pays via Paystack link
  • Split — combine any payment methods for one transaction

After payment confirmation, the receipt prints automatically and the eTIMS invoice is submitted to KRA in the background.

8
View Reports
Reports — X/Z-reports, VAT, P&L, and KRA export

Navigate to Reports in the left sidebar. Key reports for daily operations:

  • X-Report — mid-shift sales summary without resetting counters. Use this to check sales at any point during the day.
  • Z-Report — end-of-day report that resets daily counters. Print this at close of business and file with your records.
  • VAT Report — tax breakdown by period. Required for VAT returns to KRA.
  • P&L Report — revenue, cost of goods, gross profit, and expenses for a selected date range.
  • KRA Export — downloads a KRA-formatted JSON or CSV file of all eTIMS-submitted invoices. Use this for your monthly KRA filing.

All report tabs have a Export CSV button to download the data for further analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

📅

Automate your reports: Go to Settings → Automation to schedule reports to be emailed to you automatically — daily, weekly, or monthly. Never forget your end-of-month KRA report again.

🎉 You're all set!

Your Faida POS is now fully configured. You're accepting KRA-compliant sales, collecting M-Pesa payments, and tracking your inventory — all from one platform.
