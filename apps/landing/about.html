Our Story

Built to solve a real Kenyan problem

In 2024, the Kenya Revenue Authority made eTIMS compliance mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses. Suddenly, thousands of Kenyan SMEs — from Westlands cafes to Thika hardware stores — needed to submit every invoice to KRA in real time or face penalties of up to KES 1 million per year.

The existing solutions were either expensive international platforms with no M-Pesa support, or cheap local systems that couldn't handle eTIMS at all. Business owners were stuck doing manual uploads on KRA's portal — hours of extra work every week.

Faida Systems was built to fix this. We created a platform that submits to eTIMS automatically after every single sale — in the background, without any extra steps. Then we added everything else a Kenyan business actually needs: M-Pesa STK Push, offline operation for unreliable internet, FEFO inventory management, full HR and payroll, and multi-store support — all in one system, at a price any Kenyan business can afford.

Today, Faida POS is one of the most complete business management platforms built specifically for Kenya. From the 80mm thermal printer on your counter to the franchise management dashboard in your headquarters — we've built it all.
